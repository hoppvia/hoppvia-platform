[Hoppvia](https://hoppvia.com/) is an enterprise-grade, turnkey white-label ride-hailing and taxi dispatch software platform. Engineered for taxi fleet owners, private-hire operators, and mobility startups worldwide who want to launch custom-branded services with **0% commission**.

---

## 🚀 Key Modules & Capabilities

### 📱 Passenger Apps (iOS & Android)
- **100% Brand Ownership:** Published under your company's own App Store and Google Play accounts.
- **Upfront Transparent Pricing:** Dynamic dynamic fare estimation (base + distance + duration + surges).
- **Live Vehicle Tracking:** Low-latency animated GPS tracking of nearby and approaching drivers.
- **Multiple Ride Tiers:** Support for Sedan, Executive, Van/XL, and EV categories.

### 🚗 Driver Applications (iOS & Android)
- **Turn-by-Turn Navigation:** In-app route guidance with seamless one-tap transfer to Google Maps or Waze.
- **OTP Pickup Verification:** 4-digit passenger verification code prevents mismatched pickups and fraud.
- **Real-Time Earnings Dashboard:** Daily, weekly, and monthly net payouts and trip history.

### 💻 Dispatcher & Fleet Web Console
- **Automated Dispatch:** Intelligent nearest-driver matching and round-robin dispatch algorithms.
- **Geofenced Tariff Zones:** Custom pricing polygons for airports, flat-rate routes, and peak-hour zones.
- **Live Operational Map:** Bird's-eye monitoring of active, idle, and on-trip vehicles.
